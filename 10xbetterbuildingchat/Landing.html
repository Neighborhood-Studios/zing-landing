How Zing gets it done

It works inside the chat you already use with your neighbors. Nobody has to learn anything new.

1

It listens

Zing reads along quietly and remembers what matters: who neighbors recommend, what’s broken, what people are planning. Chit-chat stays chit-chat.

2

It notices

When several neighbors need the same thing, report the same problem or want the same plan, Zing spots it and offers to help, only to the people involved.

3

It organizes

Zing does the legwork: texts providers and compares prices, drafts the report, makes the event and collects RSVPs. Nothing goes out until you say yes.
