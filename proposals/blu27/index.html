Questions you're probably asking

The short answers.

$0 to the property

Nothing. No fee, no subsidy, no minimum. Residents pay per service in the app; the building simply gains the amenity. We're also open to an NOI-sharing agreement if that fits your model better — we've structured those with other multifamily management companies before.

Fully insured · W-2 employees

Zing Cleaning Inc. carries general liability and workers' compensation coverage. A certificate of insurance is provided before launch, and the ownership entity can be named as additional insured. Every professional is a W-2 employee, background-checked and trained by us — not a 1099 contractor.

Two touches from your team, total

Approving the pilot, one lobby launch morning, and one resident announcement. Zing handles scheduling, staffing, customer support, payments, insurance and quality control from there.

No master keys, ever

Residents grant access for each visit through the Zing app. No master keys, no standing access. We check in through your existing vendor process and follow building rules on service elevators and hours.

Blu27 only — no portfolio commitment

That is exactly what we recommend. Pilot Blu27 alone, measure adoption, satisfaction and operational simplicity, then decide whether it belongs across the portfolio.
