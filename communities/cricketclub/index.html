Questions

Before you sign up

Does signing up cost anything?

No. There's no card and no commitment. Your name just counts toward the 40.

How does pricing work?

Because our team is in the building every day, we can do small chores starting at $1. Full apartment cleans are among the most competitive in the market, starting at just $115 for a one-bedroom.

What happens when we reach 40?

Zing places a housekeeping team in the building that works only for residents, and the app unlocks so you can book tasks whenever you want.

What if we don't reach 40?

Nothing happens and nobody is charged. Your name stays on the list and we'll let you know how close the building is.

Who is on the housekeeping team?

Often cleaners residents already know. Zing pays them to be in the building and handles scheduling and payments through the app.

Does the building have to approve it?

We work with management before anyone starts. It's free for the property, so it's an easy yes for most buildings.
