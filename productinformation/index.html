Our cleaning products

Zing uses established household brands sold and regulated in the United States. Our disinfectants are registered with the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), which registers products that kill germs. Our other cleaners are labeled under U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission rules. Our cleaners are trained to use each product according to its label directions, and each manufacturer publishes a safety data sheet under OSHA's Hazard Communication Standard. For each product you'll find what it's for, where we use it, its main ingredient, and that safety data sheet.
